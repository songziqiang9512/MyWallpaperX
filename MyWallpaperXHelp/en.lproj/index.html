Start with a wallpaper you love

GET STARTED

A local video, a web project or a real-time scene can become your desktop. Choose your content, then make playback fit your day.

  1. Find something

    Import local videos in My Videos. Browse the Workshop for Web and Scene projects.

  2. Apply it

    Select an item in its library and choose Set as Current Wallpaper. Workshop items must finish downloading first.

  3. Make it yours

    Adjust volume and pause playback. For Web and Scene, open Properties in the item details to see the author’s options.

Video · A moving picture

VIDEO WALLPAPERS

Landscapes, animation clips and seamless loops. A video file supplies the picture, making this the most direct way to get started.

What you can do

  • Import local files in My Videos, then organize them with favorites, tags and recently used items.
  • Choose loop, sequential or random playback in settings, with automatic switching when needed.
  • Adjust playback speed and choose whether the video fits inside or fills the display.

Make it work well

  • Match the video’s proportions to your display. Fill can crop the edges; Fit can leave empty borders.
  • Pick clips with a natural transition between the last and first frames for a smoother loop.
  • The codec inside an MP4 or MOV must also be supported by macOS. Try the file in a system video player if it will not play.
Playback settings, rather than author propertiesVideos use the app’s volume, speed and playback mode settings. They do not have the author-defined property panels found in Web and Scene projects. Video speed and fill mode are specific to video.

Web · A desktop with a live view

WEB WALLPAPERS

Clocks, information displays, web animations and interactive layouts. Each project combines a web page with its resources; the author decides what it does.

What you can do

  • Filter Workshop content by Web, download a project, then play it from Downloaded.
  • Open Properties in the item details to change the colors, text, switches and other options the author exposes.
  • Supported projects can respond to the pointer or audio, when the author has included those behaviors.

Useful habits

  • Start with the default properties, then change one setting at a time.
  • Weather, remote images and other online content may need a connection, even after the project has downloaded.
  • For a blank or incomplete page, open Playback Diagnostics in the details and check the entry point, resources and dependencies.
Keep the whole project togetherA Web wallpaper is usually more than one HTML file. Do not move individual pages, images or scripts out of its folder. Presets that depend on another project need that base content too. Compatibility varies between projects.

Scene · A world built in real time

REAL-TIME SCENES

Layers, materials and animation are composed as the wallpaper runs. Supported projects can include depth, particles, dynamic text and reactive effects instead of a prerecorded video.

What you can do

  • Filter Workshop content by Scene, download an item and apply it to the desktop.
  • Use Properties to change author-provided colors, switches, values, text or image options.
  • Control playback, pause and volume through the shared controls, with energy-saving policies when needed.

Useful habits

  • Choose 30 under Maximum Frame Rate (Scene Engine) to reduce the runtime budget; 60 allows a higher frame-rate ceiling.
  • Try the default appearance before adjusting effects. Performance also depends on the complexity of the project.
  • For missing layers or unexpected effects, check Playback Diagnostics and keep the item link when reporting the issue.
Compatibility is project-specificScene compatibility is still being developed. A completed download does not guarantee every effect, script or interaction, or an exact match with Wallpaper Engine. An absent property may simply mean the author did not expose it.

Workshop & downloads

DISCOVER & MANAGE

Use your Steam account to browse projects, manage subscriptions and download wallpapers. Video, Web and Scene share this workflow.

Sign in to Steam→Search & filter→Download→Play from Downloaded

Find the right project

  • Open the Steam account control in the toolbar and follow the QR or account verification prompts.
  • Choose a content type before searching a theme. My Subscriptions shows the account’s subscribed items.
  • Open an item’s details to learn more or explore its author’s work.

Understand the queue

  • The toolbar’s download button shows queued tasks, progress and failure messages.
  • Failed items have a Retry button. Address any connection, sign-in or storage issue before retrying.
  • Clear All removes completed, failed and cancelled history. It leaves active tasks running and keeps downloaded files.
History, files and subscriptions are separateDeleting an item from Workshop → Downloaded removes its corresponding local content. Unsubscribing keeps local files. To tidy task history only, use Clear All in the download panel.

YOUR LIBRARIES

My Videos

Manage imported videos. Favorites keeps everyday choices close, Recently Used brings back previous wallpapers, and tags organize by mood or purpose.

My Images

Still images have their own library and tags. Apply an image directly when you want a quiet desktop without animated playback.

Playback, sound & energy

SHARED CONTROLS

Video, Web and Scene share play/pause, volume and mute, along with these four energy-saving policies. Set them once for all three engines.

Settings → Energy Saving & Pause
SettingWhen playback pauses
Pause when another app has focusWhen you work in another regular app. MyWallpaperX and Finder are excluded.
Pause when another app is full screenWhen the active space is occupied by another full-screen app.
Pause on battery powerWhen the Mac is running on its battery.
Pause when inactiveAfter the selected period without activity. Adjust the timeout below the switch.

Resuming playback

Playback stays paused while any enabled condition is still true. It can resume automatically once those conditions clear. A wallpaper you paused manually still needs to be resumed manually.

Sound and audio response

Volume controls the wallpaper’s own sound; it cannot add an audio track to a silent project. System-audio reactions also require a compatible wallpaper, the relevant setting and macOS permissions.

Start with a simple energy-saving setupIf you often work full screen, enable full-screen pause first. On a laptop, add battery pause as needed. The Scene 30/60 setting only affects Scene; it does not change the original frame rate of videos.

Sign-in, one prompt at a time

Steam sign-in

Start at the Steam account control. QR sign-in needs scanning and approval in the Steam mobile app. Password sign-in may then require mobile approval, an authenticator code or an email code. Follow the current prompt.

Remembering a sign-inEnable Remember me if desired. Being asked to sign in next time is not necessarily a failure if it was unchecked. An explicit “session could not be saved” message does indicate a save failure. Keep passwords, codes, QR images and session tokens out of reports.
QR code will not scan / waiting for approval

Scan with the Steam mobile app. Click the code or the enlarge button for a larger view. Refresh an unusable code and scan the new one. Switching sign-in methods cancels the previous attempt.

Confirm this sign-in in the Steam mobile app

A scanned code or accepted password may still require mobile approval. Leave the sign-in panel open and approve in Steam. Closing it or pressing Esc cancels authentication.

Previous verification code was rejected

Check whether Steam requests an authenticator code or an email code. Enter a fresh code for this attempt; old codes, typos or the wrong source can be rejected. For email, check the displayed email domain.

Sign-in failed · InvalidPassword / accessDenied

Steam rejected credentials or access. Use the Steam login account name and password, not the profile display name. Account permissions may also be involved. Check credentials and account status in the official Steam client.

authExpired / Steam token rejected

The saved session expired, was revoked or was rejected. Sign in again from the account menu, using the intended account.

Authentication timeout / service timeout / network

Authentication did not complete in time. Connectivity, a proxy, Steam availability or delayed mobile approval may be involved. Check approval and connectivity, then retry. A timeout alone does not prove a wrong password.

rateLimited / RateLimitExceeded

Steam is temporarily limiting requests. Wait before retrying instead of repeatedly refreshing codes or submitting credentials.

Signed in, but the session could not be saved

The current session is usable, but saving it failed and you may need to sign in next time. This differs from leaving Remember me off. If repeated, report the message and app version; do not start by deleting your keychain.

Steam service unavailable / incompatible version / connection lost

An unavailable or incompatible component usually requires updating or reinstalling the complete app; do not mix components from different builds. For a lost connection, restart and retry, then report the running version and source if it persists.

Make sense of a failed download

Causes & next steps

Open Download tasks and identify the failing stage: manifest download, CDN authorization, data chunks or saving local files. The same error number can call for different checks at different stages.

A message may look like this · format exampleManifest download failed (HTTP 503, node <server>, attempted <count> times). Check your network and proxy settings and try again later.
HTTP 503 / 500 / 502 / 504 · network

Possible cause

A service, content node or intermediate connection is temporarily unavailable. Browsing may work while downloads fail; 503 alone does not identify an app-version or SteamKit defect.

What to do next

Retry later. Check whether your proxy covers the app’s download connections, or compare on another network. If multiple items fail, keep the node, time and full message.

HTTP 401 · authExpired

Possible cause

Download authorization or the sign-in session is no longer valid.

What to do next

Sign in again from the account menu, then retry the failed task.

HTTP 403 · accessDenied / missing CDN authorization

Possible cause

Access was denied. Content permissions, CDN authorization or a proxy may be involved; it does not always mean a wrong password.

What to do next

Check content access, networking and proxy settings. Sign in again and retry. A staging access-denied message instead points to local folder permissions.

HTTP 429 · rateLimited

Possible cause

Steam has limited the request rate.

What to do next

Wait before retrying and avoid repeatedly creating tasks. The app already makes a bounded number of retries for some temporary failures.

HTTP 404 · unsupportedContent / item has no content manifest

Possible cause

The node lacks the content, or the item has no downloadable manifest. Updated, inaccessible or non-Wallpaper Engine content can also be unavailable.

What to do next

Reopen the item and verify the link and access. Repeated downloads cannot fix a deleted, unsupported or empty item.

diskFull · Insufficient disk space

Possible cause

The staging or destination disk does not have enough space.

What to do next

Free space on the relevant disk and retry the original task. If the message says partial data was retained, keep it for retry. Allow working space beyond the advertised archive size.

integrity · Validation failed / staging identity changed

Possible cause

Content checks, file lengths or directory identity no longer agree; moving or editing staging data can cause this. The app stops the unsafe save.

What to do next

Stop external changes to the affected folders, then retry. If repeated, report the full message and item ID. Do not edit validation data to bypass checks.

manifest requires a nonempty root project.json / manifest path…

Possible cause

Required content is missing, or manifest paths or sizes fail safety checks. A network retry alone may not resolve it.

What to do next

Verify the item is a complete Wallpaper Engine project. Report the exact error instead of assembling a folder and treating it as a completed download.

Service timeout / connection lost / helperUnavailable / protocolMismatch

Possible cause

The helper may not have started, may have exited, may be incompatible, or may not have completed a request. These messages alone do not establish a Steam outage.

What to do next

Restart for a lost connection. Update or reinstall the complete app for unavailable or incompatible components. Mention any other installed app versions when reporting.

Download stopped but staging cleanup failed

Possible cause

The task stopped or content state was saved, but temporary-file cleanup failed. This is not a request to delete the wallpaper library.

What to do next

Check the task and downloaded content state first. Keep the message and check permissions or external file operations; report persistent failures.

Wait for Completed, even at 100%Transfer is followed by validation and saving. Queued, connecting or saving tasks may not show a continuous speed. Resolve the cause before Retry. Clear all removes finished task history while keeping active tasks and downloaded files.

If a completed Web or Scene item is missing a dependency, use the Missing dependencies filter or its details to inspect the required base item. This differs from an HTTP transfer failure. Reports should include app version and running location, item link/ID, time, full error, whether one or all items fail, and network/proxy use.

When something needs a check

TROUBLESHOOTING
The wallpaper stopped moving. Has the app frozen?

Check manual pause first, then the focus, full-screen, battery and inactivity policies. Scene follows these switches too; it does not require Finder or MyWallpaperX to stay focused in order to play.

Why is a Web wallpaper blank or missing some content?

Make sure the download is complete and check Playback Diagnostics for entry-point or dependency messages. Weather, remote images and other services may be unavailable offline. A lone HTML file may not contain the entire project.

A Scene plays, but looks different from the Workshop preview.

Check your property changes against the author’s defaults and read the diagnostics. Some effects, scripts and interactions may not yet be fully compatible. Include the item link, app version, a screenshot and the affected part of the picture in your report.

A download failed with a 503 error. What should I do?

A 503 usually indicates that a service in the request path is temporarily unavailable, but the number alone does not identify the cause. Keep the error message, check your account state, connection and free space, then retry later. If multiple items keep failing, report the full error, time and app version.

Why is a wallpaper still present after clearing the queue?

Task history and local content are managed separately. Clear All keeps downloaded files. Remove a Workshop wallpaper from its Downloaded list to delete local content; unsubscribing also keeps those files.

How do I check for updates or report an issue?

Choose Check for Updates… in the MyWallpaperX menu. About MyWallpaperX shows app information. Include the wallpaper type, steps to reproduce, item link or local file format, and the error message. Never include your Steam password or verification codes.
