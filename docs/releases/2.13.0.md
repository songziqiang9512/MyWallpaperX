# MyWallpaperX 2.13.0

## 中文

2.13.0 主要更新画质控制、Scene 作品设置、原生设置窗口和五语言支持，并继续完善 Video、Web 与创意工坊体验。

- **壁纸质量**：新增低、中、高、超高四档预设，也可单独调整 10–120 FPS、MSAA ×2/×4、后处理与显示 HDR、纹理分辨率（自动/高质量/高性能）、阴影、体积光和反射。设置会显示实际生效的 mip、Bloom/HDR、阴影、反射与体积光状态；需要冷准备时提示待应用，并可重新应用当前 Scene。旧版 30/60 FPS 与 HDR 设置自动迁移。
- **Scene 作品设置**：构图、缩放、音频响应、播放速度、翻转、视差和音量现在可按作品保存，并保留作者主题色与一键恢复默认值。Scene 的画面质量与兼容性继续扩展：纹理 mip/BC、实时反射、方向光/点光/体积光、MSAA、HDR Bloom、粒子、音频、时间轴、文字与深度合成均有改进，并补充点光源和聚光灯下的模型材质表现。
- **设置窗口**：改为原生侧栏与分组表单，使用系统标题栏材质和安全区，窄窗口下仍可滚动；播放、画质、节能、外观和系统选项集中管理，新增关闭主窗口后保留 Dock 图标等界面偏好。
- **语言与帮助**：界面支持简体中文、Русский、Deutsch、Français，跟随系统语言；英文成为源语言。帮助书保留原有结构，补充画质与 Video 填充模式说明，恢复搜索并使用系统原生配色；README 提供中英两版。错误提示按错误码显示本地化文案。
- **Video、Web 与 Pixabay**：Video 默认由 AVFoundation 播放原始素材，显示真实分辨率、帧率与轨道信息，改善 HDR、宽高比、海报对齐和同窗口切换时的亮度连续性。Web 支持作品帧率建议，在加载和重载前重新播种属性，渲染进程重启期间仍可控制播放。Pixabay 显示默认可下载清晰度，并按所选清晰度下载视频。
- **底层**：Steam 获取升级到 .NET 10 与 SteamKit2 3.5，自动更新升级到 Sparkle 2.10.1，SceneScript 升级到 QuickJS-NG 0.18.0。

升级后无需重新配置。部分复杂 Scene 作品仍可能存在特效、动画或交互差异；MSAA、显示 HDR 和部分画质档取决于设备与显示器。屏保仍只支持本地视频，主动锁屏需等待系统屏保计时；多屏、HEVC/大视频和长期功耗尚未完整验证。五种语言的译文仍需母语审校，少数含多个数量占位符的文案暂无完整复数形式；在线来源受服务状态、账号权限和网络影响。

## English

2.13.0 focuses on quality controls, per-wallpaper Scene settings, a rebuilt native Settings window, and five-language support, with further Video, Web, and Workshop improvements.

- **Wallpaper quality**: Low, Medium, High, and Ultra presets, plus independent 10–120 FPS, MSAA ×2/×4, post-processing and Display HDR, texture resolution (Automatic/High Quality/High Performance), shadows, volumetric lighting, and reflections. Settings reports the mip, Bloom/HDR, shadow, reflection, and volumetric states actually in use; Scene shows a pending state when it needs cold preparation and offers a one-click reapply. Existing 30/60 FPS and HDR settings migrate automatically.
- **Per-wallpaper Scene settings**: framing, zoom, audio response, playback speed, flip, parallax, and volume are now saved per work, alongside the author theme color and Restore Defaults. Scene quality and compatibility continue to expand across texture mip/BC handling, live reflections, directional/point/volumetric lighting, MSAA, HDR Bloom, particles, audio, Timeline, text, and depth composition, including model materials lit by authored point and spot lights.
- **Settings**: a native sidebar with grouped forms, system titlebar materials, and safe areas that still scrolls in compact windows. Playback, quality, power, appearance, and system options live together, with new interface preferences such as keeping the Dock icon after closing the main window.
- **Languages and Help**: the interface supports Simplified Chinese, Русский, Deutsch, and Français and follows the system language; English is now the source language. The Help book keeps its existing structure, adds quality and Video fill-mode coverage, restores search, and uses native system colors. The README is available in Chinese and English. Error messages are localized from error codes.
- **Video, Web, and Pixabay**: Video plays the original asset through AVFoundation by default, reports the real resolution, frame rate, and track facts, and improves HDR, aspect ratios, poster alignment, and brightness continuity when switching in the same window. Web honors author frame-rate suggestions, reseeds properties before loads and reloads, and keeps playback controls available while the renderer restarts. Pixabay shows the default downloadable rendition and downloads the selected video quality.
- **Under the hood**: Steam acquisition moves to .NET 10 and SteamKit2 3.5, updates move to Sparkle 2.10.1, and SceneScript moves to QuickJS-NG 0.18.0.

No reconfiguration is needed after upgrading. Some complex Scene wallpapers may still differ in effects, animation, or interaction; MSAA, Display HDR, and some quality tiers depend on the device and display. The screen saver still supports local video only, and manual lock waits for the system screen-saver timer; multi-display, HEVC/large-video, and long-term power behavior are not fully verified. Translations still need native proofreading, and a few strings with multiple numeric placeholders do not yet provide full plural forms. Online sources depend on service status, account permissions, and network access.
